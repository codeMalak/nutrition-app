Place your app icon PNG files here before running the Capacitor iOS build.

Required files:
  icon-180.png   — 180x180 px  (iOS home screen icon)
  icon-192.png   — 192x192 px  (general PWA icon)
  icon-512.png   — 512x512 px  (App Store icon must be 1024x1024 but Capacitor handles upscaling)
  icon-1024.png  — 1024x1024 px (required for App Store submission)

Free tools to generate all sizes from one image:
  https://www.appicon.co   — upload one 1024x1024 PNG, download all sizes
  https://realfavicongenerator.net

The icon should:
  - Be a solid colored square (no transparency — App Store rejects transparent icons)
  - No rounded corners (iOS applies them automatically)
  - Your NutriTrack logo/branding centered
